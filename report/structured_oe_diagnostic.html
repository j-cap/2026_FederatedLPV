Local structured output-error estimation

Can an estimator designed for noisy states recover the local dynamics from the same fixed-speed driving maneuvers?

Design

KnownOLS is the frozen known-steering fit. AffineOLS adds a free constant offset. StructuredOE estimates the same affine dynamics by simulating the full training segment, with a fitted initial state and C=I. Only measured states and known applied steering enter estimation. Three fixed starts are selected by training loss. The configured measurement variances supply the weights.

The trajectories, state noise, speed coverage and validation maneuvers are unchanged. This is a local-estimator diagnostic, not a federated-learning result.

Outcome

OE profiles meeting the original thresholds: gentle, standard, strong. Confirmation remains paused pending review.

Failed optimizations count as failed cells. Medians and percentiles describe valid fits only; n_valid must be read alongside them.

How to interpret the metrics

matrix_error compares the six physical A/B coefficients with the simulator reference, which is never passed to the estimator. rollout_nrmse uses the historical common validation center and only A/B, allowing comparison with the frozen gates. affine_rollout_nrmse additionally evaluates each complete affine model using its training-derived offset without validation re-centering. Both use exact initial states for each one-second validation window and clean simulator states as evaluation targets; neither is an observer test.

Compare AffineOLS with KnownOLS to assess offset handling, and StructuredOE with AffineOLS to assess the estimator change. Better prediction alone does not establish matrix recovery. Inspect low speeds, convergence, and all three fleet summaries before proposing confirmation. These are reused development fleets, not independent evidence of generalization.
