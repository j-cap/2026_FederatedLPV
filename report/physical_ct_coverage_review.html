What the rank result means

Every one of the 249 single-speed fits converges with relative sensitivity rank 3. All multi-speed conditions have rank 4 at the chosen tolerance. At one speed, only s(v,μ)p₁, s(v,μ)p₂ and p₃ are determined by the local dynamics; different friction/stiffness combinations give exactly the same A/B. A converged optimizer and an interior parameter estimate do not remove that ambiguity.

The audit recomputes 10416 sampled equivalent models and verifies equality at the observed speed and the saved deviations elsewhere. These alternatives are never selected by test error. Their deviations are model differences, not confidence intervals. Single-speed extrapolation from the selected solution can depend on initialization, bounds and numerical selection along the indistinguishable family.

Implication for the next decision

The diagnostic establishes an identification cost of restricted coverage, including a structural single-speed ambiguity. It has not yet established a practically important tracking deficit, or that shared vehicle data resolves it. Even the selected single-speed mean remains below 1% matrix error, so the controller may tolerate much of this difference.

Resume the unchanged matched control stage before adding Pacejka tires, further restrictions or a sharing method. If control remains strong, parameter ambiguity alone is not enough to motivate the paper. If relevant degradation appears, the next comparison should give local and shared structured estimators the same restricted data. Shared friction or vehicle parameters would require a physical justification; fleet-wide equality is not automatic.

These are three reused pilot fleets, with full-state measurements and a correct analytical model family. Existing unrestricted Federated results are not a fair comparator for the restricted local fits.
