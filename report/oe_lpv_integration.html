From local OE fits to full-envelope LPV models

Three reused development fleets · fixed nominal LTI acquisition · no new trajectories or OE fits

Question and scope

Does learned grouping combine accurate but locally incomplete speed coverage into better full-envelope models? The primary comparison uses the same 249 saved noisy-state, known-applied-steering OE targets for every deployable method. References and family labels do not enter their fitting or group selection.

This is a development integration result, not independent confirmation. The original zero-limiter-activation gate remains failed. The prospective amendment permits limiter activity for this comparison because actual applied steering is recorded, applied bounds and settling pass, and no cells are excluded by reference accuracy. It makes no smooth-driving or LPV-controller-performance claim.

What to decide next

Inspect paired unseen-speed errors against both local baselines and Global, smoothness sensitivity, group coverage, and the clean-state diagnostic together. A positive development result supports freezing the complete pipeline for fresh fleets; a weak or negative result should be reported and traced to local-estimation noise, grouping, or coverage. No scientific success threshold or confirmation unlock is inferred from a favorable single aggregate.

Reproducibility

All local targets, full-grid evaluation references, learned coefficients, assignments, candidate scores, sufficient-statistic communication counts and per-cell errors are saved in the three execution checkpoints. Raw trajectories are unnecessary for this stage. No family labels, unseen references, validation tracking errors or local accuracy flags select learned groups. OE affine offsets and initial states remain local nuisance parameters; only the six A/B entries enter scheduling regression.
