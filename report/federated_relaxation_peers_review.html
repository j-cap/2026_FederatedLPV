5. Decision

Keep personalized FL as a candidate, but do not claim that complementary coverage is the dominant source of its benefit. The large gain versus LocalOE mostly survives when peers all operate at the target speed. The additional coverage benefit is small, concentrated at low target speed, and not yet fully converged in two relevant conditions.

For numerical closure, continue only the two unfinished SameSpeed conditions from their saved states under unchanged criteria and evaluate their affected targets again, preserving the original experiment. Then consolidate these results before expanding the plant or launching a large study. If the conclusion remains unchanged, a defensible statement is that fleet-learned regularization can modestly improve low-speed local identification, with limited additional benefit from complementary speeds in this model/data regime.

The existing three fleets are development populations. Independent confirmation and a meaningful closed-loop consequence would still be needed for a strong FL-and-control claim. No new experiment or manuscript change is included here.
