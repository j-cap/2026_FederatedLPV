\section{10H-M reconstruction: actual frozen donor libraries}
The original 10H-M--P implementation and learned coefficients were not recovered.
This section records a fresh reconstruction from the unchanged, audited 10H-L
fold fits; it does not reproduce or replace the historical reported numbers.
For each development fleet and fitting panel, two ten-donor libraries were
selected on separate donor-validation clients. The actual fitted plants were
frozen without a full-donor refit or matrix averaging. Each held-out recipient
chose its plant using only its first-record 100-sample output prefix.

All 20 libraries passed coefficient-bound, physical-coupling, provenance,
disjoint-client and causal-assignment audits. Mean recipient percentage gains
at horizon 50 relative to the same library's donor-global plant were
49.91\% for $Q_{\rm fit}=0.01$ and 46.94\% for $Q_{\rm fit}=1$.
These are averages of recipient gains after averaging the two deployment
errors per recipient, not percentage changes in pooled fleet error. The mean
of each fleet's worst recipient gain was negative in both panels
($-18.82\%$ and $-22.03\%$). Thus mean forecast improvement still does not
establish uniformly beneficial transfer. Confirmation fleets remain sealed.
