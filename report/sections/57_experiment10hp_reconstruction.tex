\section{10H-P reconstruction: core output feedback}
The four core methods were rebuilt from audited reconstructed N libraries:
public-nominal KF/LQI, learned KF only, learned LQI only and learned Both.
The 1,600 matched linear-LPV rollouts use the historical H03 LQI costs, existing
12-second maneuvers, clipped commands, causal measured-yaw integration and the
correct DARE-prior KF gain. Noise draws are freshly declared; these are not
the lost historical P trajectories. Scalar-Q calibration and local/global
extensions were deferred to focus the bottleneck diagnostic.

Entries are mean paired fleet tracking gains in percent after averaging the
two deployments per recipient. Both improves only three of five primary-panel
broadband fleets and two of five transient fleets. Prediction gains and better
beta reconstruction still do not imply a useful controller/observer combination.

All 1,600 rollouts were finite. Independent saved-step and metric errors were
below $2.0\times10^{-15}$. Strict independent full-path replay passed for 1,545
records; 55 were sensitive to floating-point ordering under clipped feedback,
with maximum tracking-RMSE change 0.11949 degrees/s. The tolerance remains
$10^{-8}$; these failures are retained separately from saved-step integrity.
Finite trajectories and frozen-speed radius checks do not certify scheduled
stability. The next frozen diagnostic compares controller models with true
states, partial truth corrections and common-input observer replay.
