\section{Path to paper consolidation}
During development, this report favors traceability over page limits: failed
experiments, design choices, and limitations remain documented. Consolidation
begins only after the oracle decision gate and at least one convincing
non-oracle federated result. At that point the paper should center on one precise
claim, retain the decisive baselines and ablations, and move implementation detail
to supplementary material. Candidate contributions are the scheduling--structure
decomposition, an uncertainty-aware clustered federated LPV identification method,
and evidence that a small number of LPV families replaces many frozen LTI regimes.
