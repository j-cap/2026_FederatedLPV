What was controlled?

  • All source trajectory arrays verified against original hashes.
  • Original sensor fits reproduced within the declared numerical tolerance.
  • Current/next noisy state records and their quiet-segment centers unchanged exactly.
  • Known input is the applied steering after amplitude and rate limiting, not the unconstrained request.
  • Both models evaluated with the same validation center, known steering and exact initial state. The original validation score is also preserved in each checkpoint.
  • No new physical trajectories, alternative estimator, clustering, or controller experiment.

Steering can only be treated as known here because the simulator applies that limited command directly. A model of actuator uncertainty would require a separate assumption.
